Guess the Sound

Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

What this app is

Guess the Sound is a private game between friends. You record a short video, the people you send it to hear only the audio and guess the sound, and the video is revealed once they have guessed. It is currently a closed test: accounts are created by the organiser and there is no public sign-up.

What we store

  • Your email address, used only to sign you in.
  • Your username and, if you add one, your profile photo.
  • Your friends, your groups and who is in them.
  • The recordings you send, and the answers you set for them.
  • Your guesses, how many times you listened, and the points you scored.
  • A push notification token for each device you sign in on.

We do not sell your data, show advertising, or track you across other apps or websites. There is no analytics or advertising SDK in the app.

Who can see your recordings

A recording is visible only to the friends and groups you send it to. Someone who joins a group later can see that older sounds exist but cannot play or guess them. Video and audio links are signed and expire after a few minutes, so they cannot be shared outside the app. A sound can be guessed for 24 hours; after that the answer and video remain visible to the people it was sent to.

Where your data is stored

Videos are stored and streamed by Mux. Everything else — accounts, profiles, friendships, groups, guesses and scores — is stored by Supabase on servers in the European Union. Push notifications are delivered through Expo's push service, which receives only a device token and the notification text.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile and tap Delete account. This removes your sign-in, email address, profile photo, friendships, group memberships and every recording you have sent. It cannot be undone.

Guesses you made on other people's sounds stay in their history and on that week's group leaderboard, shown under an anonymous name such as “deleted_4f2a…”, so that other people's own history and scores do not change.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are only created for people the organiser invites.

Changes
